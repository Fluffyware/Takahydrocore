Life at THI

Real project exposure. Real technical growth.

At THI, career growth happens close to the work: vessel decks, project preparation, field data, safety routines, and the people who keep every scope moving with discipline.

01 / Working Environment

Field-minded, team-led

A practical workplace for people who like clear preparation and hands-on coordination.

THI work connects many disciplines: geotechnical, geophysical, QHSE, equipment, logistics, commercial, finance, HR, and general support. Some people work directly on vessels and project sites. Others make sure planning, documents, data, purchasing, and communication stay organized from the office.

Site exposure Technical learning Team coordination Safety routines
Daily rhythm

Briefing, preparation, execution, checking, documentation, and reporting.

02 / Core Values

SIGAP culture

Five simple values that guide how people work at THI.

SIGAP keeps the culture practical: serve well, keep trust, keep learning, stay aware, and work professionally in every project condition.

SIGAP

S / Service Excellence

Proactive. Exceed customer expectations.

I / Integrity

Trustworthy. Committed. Responsible. Keep your word.

G / Grow

Continual learning. Adaptive. Innovative. Deliver better output.

A / Awareness

Respect diversity. Protect company reputation. Give recognition.

P / Professionalism

Work ethic compliance. Do the best. Teamwork. Lead by example.
