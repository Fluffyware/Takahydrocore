Departments

Find where your work fits inside THI.

THI project delivery depends on field crews, technical teams, project control, business support, and people operations working in the same rhythm.

01 / Department Directory

Department areas

Each department supports a different part of the project chain.

01 / People operations

Human Resources

Supports people administration, recruitment, training, compensation, benefit, and organizational development across office and field teams.

RecruitmentTrainingCompensationPeople development
Department roles

HR Manager, Compensation & Benefit Supervisor, People & Organizational Development Supervisor, Compensation & Benefit Staff, Recruitment & Training Staff.

Typical focus

Keeping recruitment, employee administration, benefits, training schedules, and people support organized.

02 / Company support

General Affair

Handles general office support, legal and project administration, warehouse coordination, logistics, and IT support for daily operations.

LogisticsWarehouseLegal adminIT support
Department roles

GA Manager, Logistic & Warehouse Supervisor, General Affair Supervisor, Legal & Project Staff, Logistic Staff, Warehouse Staff, Warehouse Administration, Logistic Administration, General Affair Staff, IT Engineer.

Typical focus

Keeping facilities, logistics, documents, warehouse records, and internal support services reliable.

03 / Field execution

Geotechnical Operation

Runs geotechnical field execution across drilling, CPT, sampling, surveying, field operation, and data processing activities.

DrillingInsitu testSurveyingData processing
Department roles

Geotechnical Operation Manager, Shallow Geotechnical & Insitu Test Supervisor, Drilling Supervisor, Geotech Engineer, Surveyor, Driller, Assistant Driller, Roughneck, Geotechnical Operator, Geotech Data Processor.

Typical focus

Preparing methods, operating field equipment, recording site progress, and controlling investigation workflow.

04 / Survey and data

Geophysical

Supports geophysical survey work through acquisition, survey operation, positioning support, and technical data review.

SurveyAcquisitionGeophysicsSurvey engineering
Department roles

Geophysical Operation Manager, Geophysical Surveyor, Geophysicist, Survey Engineer.

Typical focus

Operating survey instruments, checking acquisition quality, monitoring data, and coordinating with field teams.

05 / Workshop readiness

Facility

Keeps workshop activity, tools, technicians, and mechanical or electrical support ready for project and field requirements.

WorkshopMachiningWeldingMaintenance
Department roles

Facility Manager, Workshop Supervisor, Machinist, Welder, Mechanic, Mechanical Technician, Electrical Technician, Tool Keeper.

Typical focus

Supporting equipment readiness, workshop preparation, tool control, repair, and technical maintenance.

06 / Technical delivery

Engineering

CADInspectionMechanicalElectrical
Department roles

Engineering Manager, CAD Supervisor, Engineering Supervisor, Inspection Supervisor, Drafter, Mechanical Engineer, Electrical Engineer, Inspector.

Typical focus

Preparing drawings, checking technical requirements, supporting inspection, and reviewing engineering readiness.

ContractProject supportBid supportAdministration

HSEQMSQAParamedic

FinanceAccountingPurchasingData analysis
